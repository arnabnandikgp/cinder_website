# Cinder

Source of truth: `cinder_design_system/` and its approved assets.

- Cobalt: #0051FE. The brand accent. In the trading workspace, semantic green (#46D6A1) and red (#FF7085) distinguish bids/buys and asks/sells, respectively; they are not additional marketing accents.
- Black: #0B0B0B. Charcoal: #1F1F1F. Dark surface: #252A2E.
- Off white: #FAFAFB. Light gray: #F4F6F8. Graphite: #6B7280.
- Direction: a private financial institution; precise, restrained, confident.
- Positioning: “Your prime broker for perps.” Lead with the brokerage benefits, not the chain; the hero’s bottom-right corner and landing footer credit “Built on Solana” with its logo. This is blockchain-neutral messaging, not a claim of existing multichain support.
- Typography: self-hosted Manrope on the marketing page. Semibold headings and less compressed tracking give the messaging presence; primary descriptions are 18–20px on desktop and 17–18px on mobile. Important diagram details use 14–16px rather than miniature technical labels. The trading workspace retains its Arial-style UI sans-serif and compact technical annotations.
- Marketing surfaces: system-default light/dark themes, off-white or graphite backgrounds, cobalt accents, rounded controls (12px) and showcase panels (24px). The terminal stays dark and information-dense.
- Marketing density: comfortable, not terminal-dense. Section padding scales from 64px to 96px per end; related headings, copy and illustrations stay grouped. Diagrams occupy their panels with meaningful visual weight instead of small content surrounded by empty space.
- Theme depth: off-white reading areas alternate with pale cobalt Trading and Account sections; white/graphite agent and brokerage panels contrast with richer ice-blue private-account boundaries and selected states. The hero uses custom angular architectural planes derived from the mark, with cobalt facets, ice-blue or graphite surfaces, and a quiet central reading aperture. No WebGL, stock shader or oversized logo watermark.
- Use the exact approved logo assets. Oversized crops may be used as background artwork, never as a replacement for the intact primary logo.
- Architecture and financial relationships are native HTML/SVG. Cobalt indicates the selected execution path and private account boundary.
- Motion explains order flow, product interaction and account context. The hero's subtle architectural drift pauses offscreen and when the browser is hidden; the same complete server-rendered SVG remains static for reduced motion or no JavaScript. Muted product recordings pause offscreen and have a manual pause control; the five-second account sequence stops on interaction. Reduced-motion users see static states.
- The brokerage diagram continuously compares neutral direct connections with cobalt Cinder-routed flow, using labelled Today / With Cinder states and a legend. An open, staggered cluster of person and bot silhouettes represents participants under one “Traders & agents” heading, without numbered profile labels or avatar boxes. Its quicker forward transformation holds the final result before smoothly returning; it pauses offscreen and has no playback controls. People and agents remain outside the private-account boundary; account records stay individual, and qualifying volume aggregates separately at each venue. The final architecture is the no-JavaScript and reduced-motion state.
- The account walkthrough uses an expanding active description and a vertical timed progress rail on each item. Deliberate selection stops automatic advancement; offscreen or unfocused-browser time does not consume the current step’s remaining time.
