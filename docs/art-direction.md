# Cinder art direction

The visual direction follows the approved cobalt (#0051FE), black, charcoal, and off-white palette. Large editorial type, precise rules, restrained color, and cropped approved logo forms give the page an institutional financial character.

## Generated artwork

- File: `public/art/cobalt-architecture.png`
- Method: built-in image generation tool, September 22, 2026.
- Role: decorative hero atmosphere, displayed with reduced opacity behind the interface.
- Size: 1536 × 1024 pixels.
- The artwork is not a replacement logo. Primary logos and decorative logo crops use the original supplied assets.
- Diagrams, labels, paths, account boundaries, and animations are native React/SVG components.

## Exact generation prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: atmospheric backdrop for a premium institutional finance website called Cinder. Create a very restrained macro architectural abstract image: two immense, solid, precision-machined dark graphite angular brackets and a single cobalt-blue (#0051FE) flat diagonal parallelogram slab, cropped dramatically by the right and upper edges. This is atmospheric abstract geometry, not a logo; no text, symbols, graphs, labels, arrows, particles, stars, neon tubes, or UI. Matte obsidian surfaces, sharp chamfered edges with fine blue grazing light, rich near-black (#0B0B0B) background. Composition: landscape 3:2, left 55 percent nearly empty pure near-black fading cleanly into the geometry on the right. Geometry anchored upper-right, understated cobalt spill, architecturally precise, exclusive private brokerage aesthetic, elegant editorial art direction. No glossy chrome or generic futuristic rings. Keep most of the image very dark so code-native text and diagrams can layer over it. Render at high quality.
